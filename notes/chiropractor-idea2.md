# Idea 2: Chiropractic — Runner-up to Med Spa

Status: research phase, explored via Semrush MCP on 2026-09-14. Runner-up to
[[med-spa-idea1]] in the ranked top-5 verticals; has a warm first-client lead
(Dr. Joshua Fern, solo practice) which lowers the cold-start risk relative to med spa.

## Why chiropractic ranked #5 originally, then moved up on closer look

- Strong, suburb-durable local demand: chiropractor denver = 2,400/mo (tied with dentist,
  ahead of med spa), and unlike med spa it holds up in the suburbs — chiropractor arvada co =
  110/mo vs. med spa arvada co's 10/mo. "chiropractor near me" = 246,000/mo nationally.
- Owners are already demonstrably searching for this kind of help — the clearest "help me"
  signal of any vertical checked (chiropractor seo = 2,400/mo, chiropractic answering service =
  590/mo — see below)
- High CPC across consumer and B2B terms ($20-29) signals real ability/willingness to pay,
  similar tier to med spa
- **Have a warm contact:** Dr. Joshua Fern, solo chiropractic practice — real potential client
  #1 without needing cold outreach, sidesteps the inbound-only distribution constraint entirely
  for a pilot.

## Local consumer demand (Denver metro, Semrush US database, 2026-09-14)
- chiropractor denver: 2,400/mo (same tier as dentist)
- chiropractor arvada co: 110/mo — meaningfully better suburb retention than med spa
- chiropractor near me (national): 246,000/mo
- sports chiropractor denver: 40/mo | chiropractic adjustment denver: 210/mo

## Competitive landscape (who ranks for "chiropractor denver")
- Fragmented, independent practices — denvercoloradochiropractic.com (#1),
  shiftchiropractic.com (#2), carechiropractic.com (#3), littlemountainchiro.com,
  100percentchiropractic.clinic, reinhardtchiropractic.com — no chain dominance, same
  case-study-friendly pattern as med spa.

## What chiropractors search for help with
- chiropractor seo: 2,400/mo (CPC $24.34, competitive density only 0.08 — wide open, same
  pattern as med spa's low-density commercial terms)
- chiropractor marketing: 1,000/mo (CPC $20.67) | chiropractic marketing agency: 590/mo
  (CPC $19.90) | chiropractic website design: 480/mo (CPC $21.11)
- chiropractic lead generation: 140/mo | chiropractic consultant: 40/mo
- Avoid: "chiropractic software" (1,300/mo, CPC $21.91 — ChiroTouch/established practice-
  management incumbents) and "chiropractic crm" (0 volume)

## Important difference from med spa: "answering service" is already contested

Unlike med spa (where "med spa answering service" had 0 search volume), chiropractic has real,
priced demand at this exact intersection:

- **chiropractic answering service: 590/mo, CPC $28.86** (highest CPC of any B2B term checked
  in this vertical), competitive density only 0.04
- The SERP for this term is already crowded with national players who have dedicated
  chiropractic landing pages: wellreceived.com, myreceptionist.com, moneypenny.com,
  answermti.com, mapcommunications.com, smith.ai
- **Multiple AI-native competitors already exist specifically for this niche:**
  myaifrontdesk.com ("AI answering for chiropractic offices"), chirobasix.com ("AI front desk
  for chiropractors"), agentzap.ai. Med spa had zero competitors at this intersection —
  chiropractic already has at least three.
- chiropractor ai (50/mo) and ai for chiropractors (50/mo) both show non-zero, slowly rising
  volume — more established awareness of the AI-answering concept in this vertical than in
  med spa, which cuts both ways: validates the pain point is real and already being sold
  against, but means differentiation has to be sharper (can't just "be first").

## Read on the tradeoff vs. med spa

| | Med spa | Chiropractic |
|---|---|---|
| Local Denver volume | 1,000/mo | 2,400/mo |
| Suburb durability | Weak (Arvada = 10/mo) | Better (Arvada = 110/mo) |
| "Help me" search signal | Strong, no AI competitors | Strongest, but 3+ AI-native competitors already selling into it |
| CPC / ability to pay | High ($17-56) | High ($20-29) |
| Warm first client | None yet | **Yes — Dr. Joshua Fern** |

Chiropractic has bigger and more durable local demand and a real warm lead, at the cost of
entering a niche where the AI-answering angle specifically is already being sold by national
competitors — so the pitch needs to win on differentiation (personal/local relationship,
integration quality, price, bundled marketing) rather than "nobody else offers this."
