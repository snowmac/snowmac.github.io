# Idea 4: Other verticals checked — personal injury law, dermatology, property management

Status: research phase, explored via Semrush MCP on 2026-09-14. Rounds out the original top-5
volume scan (after [[med-spa-idea1]], [[chiropractor-idea2]], [[auto-repair-idea3]]). Grouped
into one file since all three are being deprioritized/ruled out rather than pursued further —
see conclusion at the bottom for the full six-vertical comparison.

## Personal injury law — massive dollar signal, but the most entrenched market checked

### Demand
- personal injury lawyer denver: 2,900/mo, CPC **$235.49** (highest of anything checked in this
  entire project)
- law firm marketing agency: 3,600/mo (CPC $43.47) | legal answering service: 2,400/mo
  (CPC **$133.39**) | attorney answering service: 1,600/mo (CPC **$162.07**) | personal injury
  law firm seo: 590/mo (CPC **$120.83**) | personal injury lawyer marketing: 1,900/mo
  (CPC $91.65) | law firm lead generation: 390/mo (CPC $34.05) | legal intake software: 480/mo
  (CPC $63.45, incumbent SaaS category — avoid) | ai for law firms: 880/mo | ai intake for law
  firms: 40/mo

### Why it's a pass (for now)
Checked organic rankings for "attorney answering service" — the market is decades-deep and
extremely well-funded: answeringlegal.com, lexreception.com, vezareception.com,
alertcommunications.com, ruby.com, backofficebetties.com (several are dedicated legal-answering
brands). **State bar associations (Florida Bar, Texas Bar) list official virtual-reception
vendor partnerships** — about as entrenched and trust-gated as a market can get for a new,
unfunded solo operator. The dollar signal is real (biggest CPCs by far in this whole project),
but breaking in would need real capital or a bar-association-credibility angle, not a
scrappy/organic approach.

## Dermatology — smaller signal and a structural fit problem

### Demand
- dermatologist denver: 1,900/mo (consumer side is fine)
- dermatology marketing agency: 390/mo | dermatology seo: 590/mo | dermatology practice
  marketing: 140/mo | dermatology answering service: only 50/mo | dermatology website design:
  480/mo | dermatology lead generation: 20/mo

### Why it's a pass
Meaningfully smaller B2B signal than med spa/chiro/PI law across every term checked. Also a
structural issue distinct from the search data: dermatology has seen heavy private-equity
roll-up/consolidation into large group practices over the past decade, meaning fewer true
independent owner-operators exist to sell a small-business retainer to in the first place.

## Property management — biggest surprise, and the most contested checked

### Demand
- property management denver: 1,300/mo | property management marketing: 1,300/mo (CPC $10.08)
- **property management answering service: 2,400/mo, CPC $40.35, competitive density only
  0.10** — on paper this looks like the best opportunity in the whole project: huge volume, low
  ad competition, real known pain point (tenant maintenance calls — leaks, lockouts, HVAC
  failures — are a constant, established headache in this industry)
- property management lead generation: 320/mo | property management crm: 720/mo (CPC $13.87,
  incumbent category) | property management software: 12,100/mo (CPC $34.07 — AppFolio/
  Buildium/Yardi territory, avoid) | property management website design: 480/mo

### Why it's a pass (for now)
Checked organic rankings for "property management answering service" and it's dominated by an
entire established national call-center industry built specifically for this niche:
mapcommunications.com, answerpro.com, absentanswer.com, ambscallcenter.com, callexperts.com,
answernet.com. **evs7.com and retellai.com already have dedicated "AI answering service for
property management" content — Retell itself (the AI voice platform being considered for the
build, see ai-consulting-seo-strategy.md) is already marketing directly into this exact niche.**
That's the clearest "this space is already being worked, including by the platform vendor
itself" signal found anywhere in this research.

**Takeaway:** the two genuinely open fields remain med spa and auto repair. Chiropractic is
winnable but contested and has the warm lead going for it. Property management and personal
injury law have the biggest dollar signals in the entire project but are the hardest markets to
break into without real capital — worth revisiting later if the med spa/chiro/auto-repair
playbook proves out and there's appetite to compete harder. Dermatology is a clean pass.
